SURVIVAL GUIDE

Stay alert.

  • Move: use the left joystick.
  • Look: drag anywhere on the right side.
  • Jump: tap JUMP.
  • Flashlight: tap LIGHT to switch it on or off. Watch its battery.
  • Sprint: hold RUN to escape faster, but stamina runs out. Rest to recover.
  • Fear: high fear causes visual distortions and makes the creature more aggressive.
  • Pick up: get close to glowing power cells and tap PICK UP.
  • Weapon: tap BLAST to fire a pulse that briefly stuns the creature. You have 3 charges.
  • Inventory: tap BAG to see collected items and your pulse emitter.
  • Escape: collect all 3 cells, then find the green exit.
  • Warning: watch for red eyes and listen for nearby movement.
