A SURVIVAL HORROR CAMPAIGN

HOLLOW.

Five worlds. One hundred levels. Five Hands waiting in the dark.

Find three power cells to open the exit. Every twentieth level ends in a Hand boss. Collect scrap and upgrade your equipment between levels.
Touchscreen controls · Sound recommended

HOW TO SURVIVE

  • Use the left joystick to move.
  • Drag the right side of the screen to look around.
  • Find three glowing cells, then reach the green exit.
  • Hold RUN to sprint. Stamina recovers when you rest.
  • Tap LIGHT to toggle your flashlight. Battery slowly drains.
  • Tap BLAST to stun the creature. Bosses take damage.
  • Boss levels are 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100.
  • Earn scrap and buy upgrades after each level.
